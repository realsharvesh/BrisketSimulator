Brisket Simulator

In this game, you are Sheldon Lee Cooper and you have to feed your father George a lot of Brisket or he will get mad and turn you into brisket. In order to do this, you have to bounce the brisket off the ground. All brisket starts off raw, and every bounce will cook it. You must bounce the brisket only once, as bouncing it too much burns it, and will make George mad, which will result in him turning YOU into brisket. Use arrow keys to move, cursor to aim, and hold the space bar to throw.
